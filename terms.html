What this site is

A demonstration of what ET Hair Studio's website could look like, built by Tiyan Digital. It is not operated by the salon and does not accept real bookings, payments, or personal data.

Sample content

Services, prices, hours, review cards, and stylist bios marked “sample” are placeholders for illustration. The only verified facts used are the salon's name, address, phone number, and its 4.8-star rating across 41 reviews (Birdeye listing, November 2026). Photos are illustrative and generated for this concept.

Demo forms

The appointment-request and contact forms are non-functional demonstrations. Submitting them sends nothing anywhere and creates no booking or obligation on either side.

Acceptable use

Don't misuse the demo forms, attempt to disrupt the site, or present this concept as the salon's official website.

Changes

When this concept becomes a live site, these terms will be replaced with the salon's real booking, cancellation, and service policies.
