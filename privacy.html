Legal

Privacy policy.

Plain language, short version: this demo site collects almost nothing.

What we collect

  • Demo forms: the appointment-request and contact forms on this concept site are demonstrations — they send nothing anywhere and store nothing. Anything you type stays in your browser.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no cookies: this site sets no cookies and runs no tracking scripts.

Third parties

  • Fonts: typefaces load from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of a normal web request. No account or personal data is shared with them by this site.
  • Maps links: “Get directions” links open Google Maps in a new tab — Google's own privacy policy applies once you leave this site.
  • Booking placeholder: the sample Schedulicity link is a placeholder and goes nowhere real yet. When the salon's real booking link is connected, that provider's privacy policy will apply.

The live site

When this concept becomes the salon's real website, this policy will be updated to describe exactly what the real booking system and contact forms collect, why, and how long it's kept — before a single real submission is accepted.
